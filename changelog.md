# Changelog

## v1.0 — CourseraGrab
First public release.
- **Course library browser.** Load and browse your enrolled courses with thumbnails; click to fill the URL or double-click to start downloading.
- **List and grid views** for your course library, with a search box.
- **Live in-app progress log.** Watch downloads happen inside the app instead of a separate command window.
- **Real Stop button.** Downloads run in their own process and can be stopped cleanly, then resumed later.
- **Dark & light themes** with a one-click toggle (remembered between sessions).
- **Download history & "Open folder"** for quick access to past downloads.
- **Pick video quality and subtitle language**, and choose what to include (subtitles, quizzes, notebooks).
- **No telemetry:** the app never phones home.

The download engine is adapted from the open-source
[coursera-dl](https://github.com/coursera-dl/coursera-dl) project.
